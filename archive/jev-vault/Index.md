## 🏛️ The Three Research Contracts

### 1. [[contracts/01-discrete-coupling/README|Contract 01: Discrete Invariant Coupling & Tri-Process Hierarchy]]
* **Mission:** Unifying System 0 (microsecond NNUE accumulators), System 1 (calibrated Jev decisions), and System 2 (in-context search).
* **Core Discovery:** [[Theory/The-Law-of-Discrete-Invariant-Coupling|The Law of Discrete Invariant Coupling]] — Dense continuous modulation into Clipped ReLU causes catastrophic threshold drift; coupling must occur via discrete symbolic invariants.
* **Key Theory:** [[Theory/Discrete-Bottlenecks-and-VQ-Coupling]], [[Theory/The-Shannon-Rate-Distortion-Frontier]].
* **Benchmarks:** Frontiers P1–P8 (Mazes, Tetris, Gardner Chess, 6x6 Los Alamos Chess, 20-Hop Deduction, Countdown Math, Mini-ARC Grids, Cellular Automata).
* **Analysis:** [[analysis/Analysis_Dashboard]], [[analysis/empirical_results_master.csv]].

### 2. [[contracts/02-rlcd-decision/README|Contract 02: TypeSafe Jev Decision Foundation Model (RLCD)]]
* **Mission:** Sub-100ms typed decisions emitting `(Choice, Score, Noul)` triples without fragile JSON parsing.
* **Core Discovery:** [[Theory/RLCD-Proper-Scoring-Calibration|RLCD & Proper Scoring Calibration]] — Replacing uncalibrated RLHF with strictly convex scoring rules (Brier, RPS) eliminates sycophancy.
* **Key Theory:** [[Theory/RLCD_Physics_and_Gradient_Dynamics]], [[Theory/The-TypeSafe-Jev-Theorem]].
* **Blueprint:** [[Plans/PLAN-002-Chinchilla-Optimal-Decision-Foundation]].
* **Ecosystem Radar:** 691+ verified projects indexed on [Awesome Jev Projects](https://github.com/logicrw/awesome-jev-projects).

### 3. [[contracts/03-typed-selfplay/README|Contract 03: Typed Self-Play Pretraining & Epiplexity (Zero Data)]]
* **Mission:** Pretraining Jev decision models tabula rasa from type-valid computable structure alone (zero human/natural data).
* **Core Discovery:** [[contracts/03-typed-selfplay/SPEC|Working Spec]] & [[contracts/03-typed-selfplay/epiplexity_zoo_experiment.py|Epiplexity Zoo]] — Type-directed generation achieves 0% crashes (vs 99.3% in untyped Brainf\*ck); discovery of negative transfer ($R = -0.151$) and observer-relative structural invisibility.
* **Key Theory:** [[Theory/Epiplexity-and-Bounded-Information]], [[Literature/Self-Play Pretraining with Zero Data]].
* **Blueprint:** [[Plans/PLAN-003-Typed-Self-Play-Epiplexity-Pretraining]].
