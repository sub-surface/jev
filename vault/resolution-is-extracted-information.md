# resolution is extracted information

Under the log score, DSC = H(Y) - H(Y | q) (empirical), where q is the isotonically recalibrated forecast. That is the mutual information between the outcome and the predictor's monotone-coarsened output: the bits the predictor actually extracted. Knowledge lives in DSC, not in MCB.

**Source:** Follows from the CORP identity, since a calibrated q has S(q) = H(Y|q). See also DeGroot & Fienberg 1983.
