# nn field theory

Randomly initialised networks define field theories: the infinite-width Gaussian-process limit is a free theory, and finite width adds interactions. So 'a model simulating a field theory' is an active line of work. Width 196883 buys nothing by itself. Monster symmetry would need Monster-*equivariant* layers, for example a bilinear layer given by the Griess algebra product, and nothing yet suggests that sequences need it.

**Schur constraint (established):** a Monster-equivariant *linear* map on the irreducible 196883-dim representation is a scalar, so it has one parameter. The only rich equivariant operation is the Griess algebra product on 196884 = 1 + 196883 dimensions. A Monster net is an extreme inductive bias that pays only if the data has Monster symmetry. Its specialness is symmetry, not dimension.

**Source:** Halverson, Maiti & Stoner 2021, 'Neural Networks and Quantum Field Theory' (MLST); Schur's lemma; Griess 1982; Frenkel, Lepowsky & Meurman 1988 (V-natural is the Z2-orbifold of the Leech lattice VOA, a c = 24 field theory).

**Test / open:** Speculative: does any natural binary source have a large finite symmetry group whose equivariant predictor wins? Start small: the cyclic and dihedral symmetries of periodic sources.
