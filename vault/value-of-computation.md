# value of computation

Escalate to deliberation when the expected reduction in loss exceeds its cost (Russell & Wefald). Confidence is only a proxy: on pure noise S1 is unsure, but S2 has nothing to add. In E001 the confidence gate is wasted on noise and pseudorandom sources and useful on structured ones. The gate should therefore predict *S2's gain*, not S1's doubt.

**Source:** Russell & Wefald 1991; Lieder & Griffiths 2020; E000; E001 section D.

**Test / open:** Does a learned gain predictor beat the confidence gate at matched escalation (E002)?
