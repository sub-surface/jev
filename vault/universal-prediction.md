# universal prediction

Solomonoff's universal mixture M over all programs predicts any computable source with total excess loss <= K(source) ln 2. It is the ideal ground-floor predictor, but it is only lower-semicomputable. Practical predictors are computable approximations with a prior over a restricted class.

**Source:** Solomonoff 1964; Hutter 2005; Grau-Moya et al. 2024, 'Learning Universal Predictors' (transformers meta-trained on UTM outputs) [verify ID].
