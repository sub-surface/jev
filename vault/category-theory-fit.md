# category theory fit

Three category-theoretic descriptions fit, each with a precise job. (1) Binary sequences form Cantor space 2^w, the final coalgebra of X -> 2 x X, and predictors are measures on it. (2) Markov categories give conditioning, Bayesian inversion, sufficiency and the data-processing inequality, so they are the native home of calibration. (3) Lenses and optics give learners and backpropagation as compositional, bidirectional maps.

**Source:** Rutten 2000 (Universal coalgebra); Fritz 2020 (Markov categories); Fong, Spivak & Tuyeras 2019 (Backprop as Functor); Cruttwell et al. 2022.

**Test / open:** Open: state calibration and the CORP decomposition as Markov-category facts, with isotonic recalibration as a universal monotone coarse-graining.
