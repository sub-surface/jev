# langlands ladder

A ladder of increasingly rich arithmetic sources for the ground floor. GL(1): Dirichlet characters such as the Legendre symbol. Their reciprocity laws are class field theory, and quadratic reciprocity is the first case. GL(2): modular forms and elliptic curves, with a_p(E) = p + 1 - #E(F_p), where Wiles' modularity says every rational elliptic curve's a_p come from a weight-2 newform. Beyond that: general automorphic representations matched with Galois representations. *Program claim:* each rung is a source family whose structure is known exactly, so observers can be ranked by how many of the structure's bits they extract.

**Source:** Langlands 1967; Wiles 1995 / Taylor-Wiles; E002 section B is the GL(1) rung.

**Test / open:** Port the ledger to a_p(E) sequences and Ramanujan tau. Measure what generic, Hecke-aware and learned observers extract.
