# bounded observer information

For a *computationally bounded* observer, computation can create usable information. A pseudorandom stream has Shannon/Kolmogorov content of about its seed length, yet costs every bounded predictor about 1 bit per symbol. Computation that simulates the generator turns those bits into extractable structure. Formalisms: V-usable information, pseudo-entropy, epiplexity. This is how the data-processing inequality and 'computation creates information' can both hold.

**Source:** Xu, Zhao, Song, Stewart & Ermon 2020 (ICLR, V-information); Finzi et al. 2026, epiplexity [verify ID]; HILL pseudo-entropy; E001 'pseudorandom (LCG)'.
