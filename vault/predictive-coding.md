# predictive coding

Predictive coding (Rao & Ballard 1999; Friston) is *iterative local* inference: latent states descend a prediction-error energy, layer by layer. It is not the large-jump reconfiguration idea. Jumps come from amortised inference, where a network outputs the posterior or hypothesis directly, or from discrete model selection. Predictive coding is local; amortisation is global.

**Source:** Rao & Ballard 1999; Millidge, Seth & Buckley 2022 (review).
