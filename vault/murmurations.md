# murmurations

Machine learning has already *found* new structure in this territory. Averaging a_p(E) over elliptic curves of fixed rank and similar conductor shows oscillating 'murmuration' patterns that depend on rank. They were discovered with ML classifiers and since partly explained. It is a proof of concept that learning on arithmetic sequences can surface structure that number theorists then prove.

**Source:** He, Lee, Oliver & Pozdnyakov, 'Murmurations of elliptic curves' (2022; Experimental Mathematics 2024); Zubrilina 2023 (a proof for modular forms) [verify].
