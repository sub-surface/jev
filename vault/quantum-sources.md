# quantum sources

Bits sampled from random quantum circuits are believed hard for classical observers: pseudo-entropy with a physical origin. They are a natural 'hard source' for the zoo. Quantum *predictors* are not needed at the ground floor.

**Source:** Arute et al. 2019 (random circuit sampling); the complexity-theoretic hardness conjectures behind it.

**Test / open:** Later: add simulated small-circuit sources and check that CTW-class observers sit at 1 bit while a circuit-aware predictor does not.
