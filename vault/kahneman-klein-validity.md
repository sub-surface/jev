# kahneman klein validity

Intuition is valid only in high-validity environments with rapid feedback, and confidence does not signal its own invalidity. On binary sources, an environment's validity is its achievable DSC. E000 reproduced the failure: under shift, calibration error tripled while accuracy held.

**Source:** Kahneman & Klein 2009; E000 Q4.
