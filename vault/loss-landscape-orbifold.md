# loss landscape orbifold

Parameter space modulo hidden-unit permutations is an orbifold. Its singular points, where units coincide or vanish, create saddles and plateaus. The symmetry group of a width-n layer is S_n (times scalings), not the Monster.

**Source:** Simsek et al. 2021, 'Geometry of the Loss Landscape in Overparameterized Neural Networks: Symmetries and Invariances' (ICML).

**Test / open:** Speculative: can an optimiser step discretely *between* symmetry strata, a 'cusp-jumping' optimiser? No known algorithm.
