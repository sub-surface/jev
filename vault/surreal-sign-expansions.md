# surreal sign expansions

Every surreal born on a finite day is a dyadic rational. Every surreal has a *sign expansion*: a +/- sequence, which is a path in a binary tree. Constructing the surreals from 0 is therefore growing binary strings. With ?(x) bridging the Stern-Brocot and dyadic trees, three classical trees become one binary-sequence object. Relevance to learning: speculative.

**Source:** Conway 1976 (ONAG); Gonshor 1986.

**Test / open:** Is there a predictor whose natural hypothesis space is the sign-expansion tree, and does it beat CTW on any natural source? If not, this is an aesthetic, not a method.
