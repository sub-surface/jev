# local vs global conservation

The 2026 counterexample to the Jacobian conjecture (n >= 3) gives a polynomial map with nonzero constant Jacobian determinant, so it is locally invertible and locally volume-preserving everywhere. Yet it is not globally injective, so it loses information globally. *Local conservation does not imply global conservation.* For invertible networks and flows, a per-point Jacobian check cannot certify a lossless map. This is the precise form of 'is this process always functional / information-conserving?'
