# mediants are bayesian updating

For a Bernoulli source with a Beta(a, b) prior, the posterior mean (n1 + a) / (n + a + b) is the *mediant* of the empirical fraction n1/n and the prior mean a/(a+b), taken as unreduced count pairs. Laplace's rule is the mediant with 1/2. Pooling evidence is mediant arithmetic: Farey / Stern-Brocot combinatorics.

**Source:** Elementary Beta-Bernoulli conjugacy; mediants and the Stern-Brocot tree: Graham, Knuth & Patashnik, ch. 4.
