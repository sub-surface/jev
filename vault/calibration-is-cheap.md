# calibration is cheap

Calibration can be had for free. A base-rate forecaster has MCB = 0 and knows nothing. A randomised forecaster can be calibrated on *any* sequence (Foster & Vohra). Any MCB can be removed by a monotone relabelling. Calibration is an honesty constraint, not a measure of knowledge.

**Source:** Foster & Vohra 1998; Dawid 1982; E000 and E001 here.
